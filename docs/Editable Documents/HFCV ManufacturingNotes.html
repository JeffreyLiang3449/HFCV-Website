Aluminium Connectors

Lathe

  • Insert 1 in stock

  • Zero out lathe

    • LENGTH AND DIAMETER

  • Turn the smaller wheel to 240˚, and lathe to approx 2 in (about 20 revolutions on dial)

  • Test fit

    • It will probably not fit if you did it correctly

  • Turn wheel approx 5˚ and lathe again, test fit

  • If it fits great, if it doesn’t turn wheel a little bit and repeat

  • Go to end of material, approx 0.3 in (does not need to be precise), turn on lathe, then carve a small groove out by only turning the small wheel

    • Only do so a couple degrees

  • Go about 0.2 in above groove and cut another one

  • Get metal rasp and remove burrs on the lather

    • Trust it's a lot easier now than after

  • Hollow out the middle using the drill on the lather

    • Use the bit that’s approx 0.39 in

    • Use the cinnamon gunk (not actual cinnamon just smells like it) here it makes the lather a lot happier

  • Carefully cut the lathed portion off leaving the corresponding amount of stock for application

    • 1.300 in for vertical members

    • 2.300 in for one ended horizontal members

    • 4.400 in for double ended horizontal members

  • For double ended members, repeat process on other side

  • WHEN REMOVING BE CAREFUL METAL IS HOT

Milling

  • WEAR LONG SLEEVES, HOOD, AND COVER COLLARBONE

    • Just trust it's not pleasant when hot and sharp metal pieces are flying at your face

  • Set up connector in square holder

    • Will need the 0.75 size round holder that goes into the square block

    • Lathed connector will go in end opposite to the thing you screw on the bottom of the block

    • Will need to tighten until you can't tighten anymore with the wrench

  • If necessary, clean machine so no shavings are where the block will go

    • Use the tiny brush try to prevent flying metal pieces

  • Place block holding connector in the machine

    • make sure block is flush to the side of the clamps using the small metal square

    • Run your finger across the seam to verify that it is flush

    • Tighten

  • If necessary, put the right bit into the machine

    • Ask J to show you how

    • Need to hold the machine’s brake and turn the bolt on top using the wrench

      • I can’t reach so I usually either find Zucker’s stool or ask J

  • If necessary, raise the bed of the machine by turning the giant chrome removable crank until the connector is just below the drill bit

    • It's the left crank on the bottom of the bed

    • Give yourself a couple inches of bail room

  • If necessary, change the speed of the machine using the dial on the front

    • Ask J which speed to use

    • Want the machine on high

    • Want something around 1200

  • Zero the machine (Z-axis)

    • Ask J to show you how

    • Hit the DRO button to get to the right menu

    • Crack various wheels until connector is under the drill bit

    • Put tiny piece of paper on the top of the connector

    • Crank the giant lever on the right side

      • It looks like this:

      • (refrain from saying “Wrong lever Kronk”)

    • Crank lever until you can still pull the piece of paper out but there is a little bit of resistance

    • Turn the smaller black lever so that it locks

      • It looks like this:

    • On the screen hit “Z” and then “abs zero”

  • Use the wheels to move the connector from under the drill

  • Rotate the giant lever out and crank until the screen says -0.365 in

  • Zero the machine (X-axis)

    • Make sure the drill is a bit away from the connector, turn the machine on

    • Move the connector so that the drill takes the smallest amount of material off

    • Zero the screen (same as on Z-axis)

  • Take material off in small X-axis increments by cranking the Y-axis wheel so that the bit moves back and forth

    • J suggests going in 0.02 in increments, but I suggest somewhere around 0.04. You just have to make sure everything is tight so that the connector doesn’t rotate when you mill it flat

  • Take the correct amount of material off

    • Amount will vary depending on measurements of the individual connector

    • Leave 0.3 in of a rim for vertical and one sided horizontal connectors

    • Leave 0.2 in of a rim on each side for a double sided horizontal connector

  • Repeat on the other side

    • Make sure connector and block is going on clean, level surface

    • Make sure connector and block is flush with the clamp

    • Re-zero X-axis, you do not need to re-zero Y-axis

  • Use a metal rasp to remove the burrs

Drilling

  • Use mill machine

  • Change drill bit from milling to drilling

    • Will want something that makes a pilot hole and then a ⅜ in drill bit

  • Mark the correct spot on the flat part of the connector

  • For one ended pieces

    • Put the flat part of connector on top of the metal pieces in the clamp

      • They look like this:

    • Tighten clamp

    • Drill pilot hole, change drill bit, drill actual hole

  • For double ended pieces

    • Put the flat part of connector on smaller square aluminium tubes in the clamp

      • Use as many scrap square tubes as needed to make top clamp-able and flush-ish (try your best)

    • Use same process as one ended pieces
